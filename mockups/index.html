Hermes BotsMockups for approval

Static, clickable mockups of Hermes Bots. Sample data only. Nothing here connects to Hermes. Each screen is a phone at 390 wide and a desktop window at 1280. The two frames are separate: a tap stays inside that frame. Light, Dark, or System is one control, remembered in this browser and applied inside every frame. The look is Zoetrik. Its fonts load from Google Fonts.

    Open a frame in its own tab from the Open link if you want to click through without the gallery chrome. Serve this folder as static files. There is no build step.
