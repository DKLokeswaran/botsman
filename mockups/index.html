Hermes BotsMockups for approval

Static, clickable mockups of Hermes Bots. Sample data only. Nothing here connects to Hermes, and nothing is loaded from the network. Each screen is a phone at 390 wide and a desktop window at 1280. The two frames are separate: a tap stays inside that frame. Theme follows the system until you pick Light or Dark. That choice is shared with the frames.

    Open a frame in its own tab from the Open link if you want to click through without the gallery chrome. Serve this folder as static files. There is no build step.
